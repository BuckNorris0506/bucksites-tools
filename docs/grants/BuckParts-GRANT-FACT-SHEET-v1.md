## What BuckParts is

**Truth-first homeowner-help** for replacement filters and consumable parts—not an online store, not a marketplace, not a guaranteed-fit service. Buying options appear **only when evidence gates pass**; affiliate links are **secondary to truth**.

---

## Problem

Homeowners struggle to match **appliance model → correct filter/part**. Wrong purchases waste money and time. Compatible-looking listings and alias codes increase confusion.

---

## Solution (in one line)

**Evidence-first replacement intelligence** with confidence states, public trust pages, and **no buy button until safe**.
