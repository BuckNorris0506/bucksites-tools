## What does your organization do? (short)

BuckParts helps homeowners avoid buying the wrong replacement filter or part by showing fit evidence, uncertainty labels, and buying options only when retailer listing checks pass. BuckParts is not a store.

---

## What problem are you solving?

Homeowners often buy the wrong replacement filter because model numbers, compatible replacements, retailer listings, and fit evidence are confusing. Wrong orders waste money and delay repairs.

---

## What is your solution?

BuckParts is a truth-first homeowner-help system. It maps models to filter numbers where repo evidence exists, shows original versus compatible labels when supported, displays uncertainty instead of guessing, and opens buying options only when internal listing checks pass. Missing buy buttons are intentional when evidence is not safe enough.

---

## Who benefits?

Homeowners and family caregivers shopping for appliance replacement filters—especially people comparing model numbers, old filter labels, and retailer pages before ordering.

---

## How is this different from ecommerce?

BuckParts does not sell products, ship orders, or run checkout. Retailers handle purchases. BuckParts focuses on wrong-part prevention and decision clarity, not shopping cart conversion.

---

## How do you make money?

BuckParts may earn affiliate commissions when users click certain outbound retailer links. Those links are **secondary to truth**—shown only when listing evidence passes checks. Commissions help cover operating costs (hosting, AI/tool credits, browser verification, marketing, development). Revenue does not override fit evidence.
