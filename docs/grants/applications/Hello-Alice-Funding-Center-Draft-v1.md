## 250-word description

**Business:** BuckParts (buckparts.com — live deploy commit **UNKNOWN** unless re-verified)

**What we do:** BuckParts helps homeowners find the correct replacement filter for home appliances by comparing model and part evidence before any purchase link appears. BuckParts is not an online store, does not hold inventory, and does not run checkout. Retailers fulfill orders when a vetted outbound link is shown.

**Problem:** Wrong-part orders are common because part numbers, aliases, and marketplace “compatible” packs are easy to confuse. That wastes money and delays filter changes people need for drinking water and indoor air quality.

**Solution:** Evidence-first lookup, uncertainty labels, and buy gates. Missing buy buttons are often intentional. Affiliate commissions, where earned, are secondary to truth.
