# BuckParts Fit Lookup — Positioning Idea

**Status:** Marketing / positioning note only (not public UI copy).  
**Saved:** 2026-05-31  
**Owner:** Jared Buckman  

---

## 1. Idea

When people buy tires, windshield wipers, batteries, headlights, or oil filters for their vehicle, they do not guess. They enter their vehicle and check what fits. AutoZone, Firestone, and similar auto-parts services made fit lookup normal for cars.

BuckParts is building that same “does this fit?” layer for **home replacement parts** — starting with refrigerator filters, air purifier filters, whole-house water filters, vacuum bags, and similar home consumables.

**PROVEN:** BuckParts already publishes model- and filter-oriented lookup surfaces for several home consumable wedges (refrigerator water, air purifier, whole-house water, vacuum, humidifier, appliance air).  
**INFERRED:** Customers who already trust vehicle fit lookup will recognize the mental model quickly.  
**UNKNOWN:** Category completeness across all home consumables — do not imply full coverage.

---

## 2. Best customer-facing framing

- **Primary frame:** “Fit lookup for home replacement parts.”
- **Supporting line:** “Fit lookup for your home, not just your car.”
- **Short explanation:** “When you buy tires or windshield wipers, you don’t guess. You enter your vehicle and check what fits. BuckParts brings that same idea to home replacement parts — starting with filters.”

Do **not** position BuckParts as “the AutoZone for appliance parts.” That framing implies retailer/store/inventory ownership BuckParts does not have.

---

## 3. Phrases to use

- Fit lookup for home replacement parts
- Fit lookup for your home, not just your car
- Does this fit? — for your home, not just your car
- Enter your model / find what fits
- Wrong-part prevention through fit lookup
- Home consumables: filters, bags, and replacement parts that must match your model
- Starting with filters (refrigerator, air purifier, whole-house water, and related wedges)
- My Home Filters — personal fit context for repeat replacement (future framing)

---

## 4. Phrases to avoid

- “The AutoZone for appliance parts” (implies retail inventory / store network)
- “We sell every part for every appliance” (overclaims completeness)
- “Guaranteed to fit” without model-specific proof path
- “Full catalog” or “complete coverage” unless repo truth supports the wedge
- Any claim that BuckParts owns fulfillment, warehousing, or retail stores
- Implying all home categories are live when only proven wedges are published

## 6. Truth guard

- BuckParts is **building** this fit-lookup layer; do not overclaim category completeness.
- Cite only wedges and SKUs that repo truth / Command Center lanes prove as published or in active batch scope.
- Fit lookup is guidance and model mapping — not a substitute for OEM verification where evidence is still `SEARCH_PLACEHOLDER` or owner-review pending.
- AutoZone / Firestone references are **analogy only** — BuckParts is not those businesses and does not operate retail stores.
