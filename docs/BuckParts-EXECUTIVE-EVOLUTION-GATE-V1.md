# BuckParts Executive Evolution Gate v1

**Status:** Permanent gate — judgment standard only.  
**Authority:** Subordinate to `docs/BuckParts-CONSTITUTION.md` and the Executive Operating System (`AGENTS.md` + current stopping point in `docs/BuckParts-HQ-HANDOFF.md`). Does **not** authorize mutation, NBA, Dispatch, Daily Operator, Command Center steering, scoring, weighting, new stores, or new engines.  
**Question this gate answers:** *What evidence must exist before a proposed Executive organ becomes permanent?*  
**Burden of proof:** On the **proposer of the organ**. Silence, elegance, roadmap pressure, or reviewer convenience do not pass the gate.

---

## 1. Purpose

BuckParts must not accumulate Executive machinery that does not earn permanence through customer-facing truth or operator learning that changes decisions.

This document is the **smallest durable gate** between:

- a **proposal / experiment** (temporary, reversible, non-steering by default), and
- a **permanent Executive organ** (durable contract + attach surface + continued operating cost).

It invents **no architecture**. It scores **nothing**. It stores **nothing**. It only states what must be proven in writing before permanence is claimed.

---

## 2. Hard rules (derived)

From Constitution + Operating System only:

1. **Systems are means** — internal process earns permanence only through customer value or decision quality (§3 Customer Reality).
2. **Evidence precedes authority** — discovery is not closure; proposals are not organs (§7, §12).
3. **UNKNOWN beats invention** — missing proof is fail-closed, not a soft pass (§5, §6).
4. **One proving ground / one lane** — expansion for its own sake is prohibited (§8; OS one-lane rule; HQ “no Executive OS expansion for its own sake”).
5. **Expose → measure → correct** — permanence follows reality contact, not design completeness (HQ execution loop).
6. **No steering from unproven sensors** — sensory or drafting surfaces do not become NBA/Dispatch/Daily authority by merge alone (HQ Drift Watch F2/F4; OS founder gates).
7. **Verification ≠ customer proof** — tests/validators prove honesty of the organ’s claims, not homeowner outcomes (HQ Drift Watch F6; HyperAgent rule).
8. **Removal must stay cheap** — prefer reversible attachments; permanence that cannot be removed fails Trust Hierarchy §4 item 4 (reversibility).

**Default verdict:** `PARK` or `REJECT`. Permanence is never the default.

---

## 3. What counts as an “Executive organ”

Any proposed permanent capability that claims to be part of the Executive Operating System surface, including but not limited to:

- a new sensory path, scoreboard sibling, or Command Center field family
- a new drafting discipline, registry, queue, or decision artifact type
- a new Runner / digest / dashboard attach that changes what founders are told to do
- a new “learning,” “prior,” “weight,” “NBA,” or “dispatch” consumer

**Not an organ (gate does not apply as permanence claim):**

- a one-lane bugfix or trust repair under existing contracts
- a dry-run / owner-review packet that does not claim a new permanent surface
- documentation that restates existing Constitution/OS rules without new attach points

If unsure whether something is an organ, treat it as an organ. The proposer must clear the gate or explicitly label the work **temporary / non-permanent**.

## 4. The Gate (eight fields — all required)

Every proposal for a **permanent** Executive organ must answer all eight fields in writing.  
Empty, vague, aspirational, or `UNKNOWN` answers ⇒ **gate FAIL** (no permanence).

| # | Field | What must be proven | Fail-closed if… |
|---|--------|---------------------|-----------------|
| 1 | **Observation gained** | Exact new observation the organ makes possible that did not already exist as PROVEN repo truth. Name the signal and where it is read. | Observation is restated metrics, vanity counters, or “better visibility” without a new checkable fact. |
| 2 | **External outcome expected** | The customer or reality outcome this observation is *for* (wrong-part prevention, honest no-buy, clearer comparison, reversible correction). Internal elegance is not an outcome (§3). | Outcome is internal-only (dashboard beauty, coverage theater, agent convenience) or monetization-first (§4). |
| 3 | **Existing organ overlap** | Named existing organs/contracts this overlaps; why those cannot absorb the work; what will *not* be duplicated. | Overlap is denied without naming siblings, or proposes a parallel store/engine for the same class of fact. |
| 4 | **Constitutional necessity** | Which Constitution obligation fails if this organ does **not** exist (cite §). Prefer harm reduction, honest UNKNOWN, evidence-before-authority, fail-closed automation. | Necessity is speed, completeness, roadmap symmetry, or “Phase N expects a sibling.” |
| 5 | **Measurable learning increase** | What decision changes after the organ sees reality — and how learning is checked without a score/weight engine. Prefer: fewer repeated founder questions, fewer false certainties, clearer PARK/REJECT, honest UNKNOWN rates. | “Learning” means a score, rank, weight, or auto-NBA. Or no before/after decision difference is named. |
| 6 | **Failure mode** | How the organ fails safely: what stays UNKNOWN; what it must never steer; what false proof it must refuse (no false zero; no invented precedent; no buy CTA from soft confidence). | Failure mode is omitted, or failure would quietly grant authority / invent certainty. |
| 7 | **Removal test** | Exact test: *If we delete this organ tomorrow, what customer harm or decision-quality loss is PROVEN?* If none, it is not yet permanent. Also state how to remove (files/contracts/attach points) without stranding authority. | Removal would be expensive, or no one can name a loss — then it has not earned permanence. |
| 8 | **Graduation criteria** | Finite, checkable conditions under which a temporary experiment may be called permanent — including reality exposure (not tests alone), non-steering posture held, and founder acknowledgment. | Criteria are vibes (“feels useful”), test pass alone, or merge-as-proof. |

### Verdict labels (no scores)

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| `REJECT` | Fails constitutional necessity, invents authority, or creates unnecessary machinery. |
| `PARK` | Maybe later; evidence incomplete; do not build permanence. |
| `TEMPORARY ONLY` | May exist as experiment / drafting / read-only probe; **must not** claim permanent organ status. |
| `GATE PASS (candidate)` | All eight fields answered with checkable claims; still requires founder merge/permanence decision. Does **not** auto-apply or auto-steer. |

There is no numeric score. There is no weighted rubric. Pass/fail is qualitative and fail-closed.

## 5. Permanence vs temporary (OS alignment)

| Stage | Allowed | Not allowed |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| **Proposal** | Written gate packet (this doc’s eight fields) | Claiming the organ already exists |
| **Temporary** | Read-only / dry-run / non-steering experiment under OS Discover→Validate | New stores, scoring engines, NBA/Dispatch consumers “to learn faster” |
| **Permanent** | Founder-accepted contract + attach surface after **GATE PASS** and reality exposure | Treating merge, test green, or HQ mention as graduation |

HQ execution philosophy remains binding: **one** permanent capability → expose to reality → measure → correct → repeat.  
This gate is the written proof step before “permanent” is said.

---

## 6. Anti-accumulation clauses

1. **Burden on the organ** — reviewers need only find one unanswered field or one invented claim to withhold permanence.
2. **No twin organs** — if field 3 shows an existing organ can absorb the observation, extend that organ; do not mint a peer.
3. **No scoring theater** — any proposal whose “learning” requires a weight engine, priority score, or auto-rank fails field 5 unless and until Constitution/OS are amended to allow it.
4. **No regime shift by attach** — wiring a sensor into NBA/Dispatch/Daily is a **new** organ proposal and must clear this gate again (HQ F2/F4).
5. **Map must not eat territory** — Command Center / digest fields do not graduate a probe into permanence by display alone (HQ F4).
6. **Precedent discipline** — when arguing from past organs, cite closed precedent honestly or state `NONE` / `UNKNOWN` (Precedent Clause posture); do not invent institutional memory.
