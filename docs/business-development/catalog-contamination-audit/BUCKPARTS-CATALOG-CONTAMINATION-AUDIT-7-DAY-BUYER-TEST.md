## 7. Success criteria (validate D thesis)

The **D — Wrong-Part Intelligence Company** thesis is **validated** if, within 7 days of sends:

| Signal | Threshold | Interpretation |
|--------|-----------|----------------|
| **Explicit pay intent** | ≥ **2** prospects say **Yes** or **Maybe** with a concrete next step (scope call, RFP, budget band) | INFERRED — willingness to pay for audits exists |
| **Problem recognition** | ≥ **5** prospects acknowledge catalog contamination / fitment data quality as a real cost (even if not buying now) | INFERRED — problem is legible to market |
| **Meetings** | ≥ **3** meetings held or scheduled with qualified buyers | INFERRED — sales motion is viable |

**Strong validation (upgrade confidence):**

- ≥ **1** prospect names a **price band** or past spend on catalog hygiene / data quality.
- ≥ **1** prospect asks for audit on **their** catalog without BuckParts prompting a discount or free pilot.

---

## 8. Failure criteria (kill or pause D thesis)

The D thesis is ** weakened or killed** if:

| Signal | Threshold | Interpretation |
|--------|-----------|----------------|
| **Zero pay intent** | 0 Yes / 0 Maybe with next step after 10 sends + one follow-up | INFERRED — market may not pay for audits at this framing |
| **Zero meetings** | 0 meetings scheduled | INFERRED — outreach or ICP misfire |
| **Uniform rejection reason** | ≥ **7** cite same blocker (e.g., "we do this in-house," "no budget," "not our problem") | INFERRED — reposition or change buyer persona |
| **Sample confusion** | ≥ **3** prospects misread sample as accusation or legal claim | **Process failure** — fix disclaimers before continuing |

**Pause (not kill)** if sample generates interest but prospects demand free full-catalog audit first — indicates interest without monetization path unless scope is tightened.

## 9. What result means for strategic fork

| Outcome | Recommended interpretation |
|---------|---------------------------|
| **Validated** | Double down on D: productize Catalog Contamination Audit SKU; keep A (Compatibility Infrastructure) as data/API layer beneath audits. |
| **Mixed** | Refine ICP or packaging (e.g., retailer-only, family-scoped pricing); rerun test with 10 new prospects before building more tooling. |
| **Killed** | Do not build audit sales motion; revisit D only if a inbound pull appears. Continue A preservation via internal verification work only. |

This test does **not** authorize building B (Home OS) or pursuing C (Agentic Commerce Verification) as near-term revenue.
