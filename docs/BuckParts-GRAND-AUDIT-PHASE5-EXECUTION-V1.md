## 3. Ranked constraints

**Q1 — the five biggest constraints to $10M+ (ranked):**

1. **Zero demand** (INFERRED on PROVEN substrate): no measured arrivals; young domain; thin content (standing finding); SEO-only plan in an AI-compressed SERP era. Supply truth exists; nobody is arriving to use it.
2. **Monetization rails approved but not activated** (PROVEN): two approved networks, yet the 41 OEM-class fridge links earn nothing and no approved-network link is live on a proven path. Revenue-per-visit is ~zero even if traffic appeared.
3. **Wrong unit of scale** (PROVEN gap): customers decide in model-space (500 models, 907 mappings); the site answers in product-page space (~178). The differentiated asset — the wrong-part negative dataset — is not a customer-facing answer surface at scale.
4. **No B2B productization of the data asset** (INFERRED): the plausibly-$10M path — verified fit-graph + wrong-part prevention licensed to retailers/warranty/insurance — has zero probes run. Affiliate economics alone (~$2/order class commissions) require traffic volumes implausible for $10M on this catalog; enterprise value likely needs the data-asset layer, with the consumer site as its proving ground.
5. **Founder-throughput ceiling** (PROVEN pattern): one person is the actuator for every approval/mutation/content decision; the loop is safe but content/coverage production has no factory cadence yet.

## 4. Ranked execution priorities

**Q2 — attack first:** **Demand (constraint 1) by effort share; rail activation (constraint 2) first chronologically** — activation is days of finite work with existing machinery (see Q8), demand is the compounding months-long pole that everything else needs. Constraints 3-5 are attacked THROUGH the demand program (model-answer pages ARE the unit fix; the B2B probe rides on demand evidence; throughput is solved by batch cadence, not tooling).

### Freeze / justify (from source Q4–Q5)

**Q4 — freeze (the instrumentation moratorium, now in force):** new CC lanes/siblings; new validators/report scripts/manifest classes/sentinel frameworks; driverless refactors of proven read-only lanes; new wedge/category expansion (nothing new until the fridge head works); routine three-auditor ceremony for CONTENT work (reserve the full chain for mutations and releases); any dashboard/neuron expansion.

**Q5 — still justified:** (a) clearing the two completion exceptions (env restoration; sanctioned signature re-capture) — hours, closes Phase 4 formally; (b) data-into-lanes operations (GSC/GA4 flowing; receipts); (c) OPERATING the Phase 3 parity-correction loop — execution, not instrumentation — including its new highest-value use: **rail activation** (below); (d) affiliate link integration when approvals convert; (e) the small hygiene backlog; (f) content-production tooling ONLY after manual production proves a specific bottleneck (prove-need-first).

### Highest-ROI work by category (from source Q6–Q9)

**Q6 — customer work, highest ROI:** model-level fit answers on the head ~100 fridge models — BUY / DON’T-BUY / honest-UNKNOWN per model with wrong-part warnings drawn from the documented negative dataset (GSWF 100% wrong-part family, EDR cohorts, HAF-QIN mismaps). This is simultaneously the demand play (model-number queries), the differentiation play (nobody else says “don’t buy X”), and the unit-of-scale fix. Second: failure-path honesty pages (no-safe-path → trusted OEM handoff).

**Q7 — business work, highest ROI:** the **five-conversation B2B probe** (warranty providers, parts retailers, appliance-repair networks: would you pay for verified fit + wrong-part prevention data?) — the cheapest possible test of the $10M thesis; plus external demand-data acquisition (installed-base/search volume for the head-model ranking) and pushing the four in-review affiliate applications to resolution.

**Q8 — monetization work, highest ROI:** **activate the approved rails on proven paths** — map the 41 unmonetized OEM-class links to Rakuten/Amazon(/CJ/ShareASale when approved) merchant equivalents, and upgrade rows **through the Phase 3 parity-correction loop**: founder-approved, single-row, evidence-gated URL corrections — the loop built for drift repair is precisely the safe rail-activation mechanism. Evidence-gated Amazon re-enablement where browser proof supports it. Target: first tracked converting handoff within 30 days.

**Q9 — distribution work, highest ROI:** (1) answer-seeding where model-number questions already live — repair forums/Reddit with disclosure (high intent, zero domain-authority requirement); (2) wrong-part-story short video (evergreen, search-adjacent, uses the existing brand kit); (3) partnership placement with appliance-repair publishers/newsletters; (4) email capture on model pages from day 1 (“save your model” — the My Home Filters seed; compounding, owned). All four are measurable in the demand/outcome lanes that now exist.

### Five voices (from source Q10 — all INFERRED)

**Q10 — what each would say (all INFERRED):**
- **Amazon:** “Get a buy path that converts and measure it. Returns are the only fit-truth that matters — until you can see them, optimize handoffs from confident decisions. Stop polishing internal truth.”
- **McMaster-Carr:** “Your data IS the product. Go deeper per model — specs, cross-references, supersessions — on fewer items, and make lookup instant. Time-to-correct-part is your metric.”
- **Costco:** “Curate ruthlessly. 100 models done perfectly with membership-grade trust beats 100,000 done thin. Say no to new categories.”
- **Google:** “End the search. Model+symptom pages that answer completely — that’s what earns rank and the AI-overview citation. Thin affiliate pages die in our index.”
- **Stripe:** “Obsess the first transaction. Instrument-to-first-dollar is done — now make the first tracked converting handoff happen this month, then make it repeatable.”

## 5. 90-day roadmap

**Ranked 90-day execution roadmap (Q3)**

**Days 1–7 — unblock everything cheap:**
1. Clear the two Phase 4 completion exceptions (env vars; sanctioned signature re-capture) → chain green → formal declaration.
2. Rail activation sprint: inventory Rakuten/Amazon merchant coverage for the 41 OEM-class links; queue the first 5-10 evidence-gated link upgrades through the parity-correction loop (founder-approved, single-row).
3. Acquire external demand data (installed base + search volume); rank the head-100 fridge models.
4. Confirm GSC/GA4 telemetry flowing into the demand/outcome lanes (weekly digest as the canary).

**Days 8–30 — the answer factory, sprint 1:**
5. Ship 25-30 model-level fit-answer pages (head models; BUY/DON’T-BUY/UNKNOWN + wrong-part warnings) with email capture.
6. Publish 5-8 wrong-part prevention deep pages from the negative dataset (the differentiated content class).
7. First tracked converting handoff on an activated rail (Stripe test).
8. Begin answer-seeding in 2-3 repair communities; record which pages get cited.

**Days 31–60 — scale what works, open the second front:**
9. Sprint 2: +40-60 model pages, prioritized by early GSC/impression signal.
10. Launch the video/partnership distribution tests (3 cheap parallel experiments).
11. Run the five B2B conversations with a one-page fit-data/wrong-part-prevention offer; write down verbatim objections and willingness-to-pay.
12. Push CJ/ShareASale/Walmart applications to resolution; widen rail coverage on shipped pages.

**Days 61–90 — concentrate and decide:**
13. Double down on the single strongest demand channel per outcome-lane evidence; complete the head-100.
14. Convert every approved rail onto every proven head page (all founder-gated through the loop).
15. **Day-90 fork (the audit’s output):** with 90 days of arrivals, handoffs, and B2B conversation evidence — weight the next quarter between consumer-affiliate scaling and the B2B data-asset productization. Enterprise value is maximized by arriving at this fork with BOTH signals measured, which the Phase 4 quartet now makes possible.
