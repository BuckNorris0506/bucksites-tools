# BuckParts Constitution

**Status:** Governing document — durable principles that bind founders, operators, contractors, and automated systems.  
**Scope:** Judgment and values. Does not replace law, safety requirements, or explicit documented founder override.  
**Subordinate documents:** Customer language guides, operational handoffs, and implementation specs implement this Constitution; they do not supersede it.

---

## Preamble

This Constitution defines what BuckParts is allowed to become — not how any particular week’s work is executed.

When this Constitution conflicts with tactical documents, **this Constitution wins**. When this Constitution conflicts with measurable targets, coverage goals, or internal convenience, **this Constitution wins**.

Amendments require founder approval and a brief recorded rationale. Material changes to trust-related commitments require the same.

---

## 1. Mission

BuckParts exists to help people **finish a replacement without buying the wrong part**.

BuckParts is a **decision utility under uncertainty** — not a catalog, not a storefront, not an affiliate blog. Its core job is to decide whether **enough evidence exists to justify an action**, including when humans must still act without complete certainty.

The first obligation is **harm reduction**. Traffic, revenue, and catalog breadth are secondary to wrong-part prevention.

BuckParts earns the right to recommend action only when evidence supports it.

---

## 2. Customer Promise

Every homeowner interacting with BuckParts can expect the following:

- BuckParts is **not the seller**. Checkout always happens elsewhere.
- BuckParts will **not invent fit** to fill gaps or improve conversion.
- BuckParts will **show uncertainty** when uncertainty exists.
- BuckParts will **withhold buying guidance** rather than imply a safe purchase without evidence.
- BuckParts will say **what to compare** — part number, label, manual — before order.
- BuckParts will not make blanket claims of **guaranteed fit**, **safe to buy**, or **fully verified** products.

---

## 3. Customer Reality Doctrine

**Purpose:** Keep BuckParts oriented toward outcomes people experience, not internal sophistication.

- **Customer outcomes outrank internal elegance.** A simpler system that prevents wrong parts beats an elegant system that confuses or misleads.
- **Systems are means, not ends.** Infrastructure, process, and tooling exist to serve homeowners — not the reverse.
- **Internal processes must justify themselves through customer value.** A workflow, review step, or document that does not improve clarity, safety, or decision quality for the homeowner does not earn permanence.
- **What homeowners see must reflect what we actually know** — not what we wish we knew, what we are building toward, or what would improve internal metrics.

---

## 4. Trust Hierarchy

When goals conflict, resolve them in this order:

1. **Physical fit truth** — shipping the wrong part is the worst outcome.
2. **Honest representation** — what we show must match what we know.
3. **Homeowner comprehension** — clarity beats cleverness.
4. **Reversibility** — prefer actions that are easy to undo or correct.
5. **Monetization** — referral or affiliate paths are **secondary and gated**; they never outrank items 1–4.
6. **Operational velocity** — speed never overrides items 1–4 on public buy guidance.
7. **Coverage breadth** — publishing or indexing thin pages does not justify weak buy paths.

---

## 5. Truth Contract

**Purpose:** The standing agreement between BuckParts, its people, its systems, and its customers about how truth is handled.

- **Reality outranks opinion.** Preferences, narratives, and incentives do not change facts.
- **Evidence outranks memory.** What can be checked beats what someone recalls.
- **Unknown is preferable to invented certainty.** Absence of proof is a valid state; fabrication is not.
- **Claims should be traceable to evidence.** Public statements about fit, product type, or buying options must be supportable from committed evidence on that surface.
- **Corrections improve truth.** When evidence changes, BuckParts updates representation — including retracting or suppressing prior guidance.

The Truth Contract applies to humans and automated systems equally.

---

## 6. Uncertainty Doctrine

Uncertainty is central to replacement shopping — similar model numbers, alternate codes, listings that look right but are not.

- **FULL truth or UNKNOWN** — BuckParts does not ship partial-confidence claims to homeowners (“probably fits,” “mostly verified,” “good enough”).
- **UNKNOWN is a valid public state** — not a failure to hide.
- **No-buy is a trust feature** — withholding a store link when evidence is weak is success, not embarrassment.
- **Demand is not proof** — interest, traffic, or commercial opportunity never substitutes for fit or listing evidence.
- **Silence over speculation** — if a claim cannot be supported from committed evidence on that page, BuckParts does not imply it.
- **Human judgment remains legitimate** — BuckParts assists comparison; the homeowner still confirms against their old part or manual.

---

## 7. Evidence Standards

**Purpose:** What earns public claims and buy authority.

- **Evidence precedes action authority** — buying options are earned, not assumed from inventory rows alone.
- **Source-backed claims only** — fit, listing, and product-type claims trace to checkable sources.
- **Separate dimensions explicitly:** fit mapping, listing check, product quality, warranty impact, and performance claims are distinct; none implies the others.
- **Point-in-time checks are not permanent truth** — listing verification must not be presented as timeless certainty without an explicit refresh policy.
- **Compatible is not official** — third-party replacements must not imply brand manufacture or endorsement.
- **Commercial relationships are not product proof** — partnership or referral eligibility does not validate fit or quality.
- **Discovery is not closure** — candidates produced by research or automation require validation before they become public authority.

---

## 8. Product Philosophy

- **Answer-first, not browse-first** — pages exist to resolve a specific replacement question.
- **Authority engine, not inventory engine** — the product decides whether action is justified.
- **One proving ground at a time** — expansion into new problem domains requires demonstrated proof in the current domain, not opportunity alone.
- **Restraint over hype** — no superlatives, checkmark theater, or “magic” claims.
- **Public surfaces graduate; internal systems learn** — weak-confidence inventory belongs in internal queues, not public buy guidance.
- **New domains are not implied** by current replacement work unless explicitly chartered and constitutionally amended.

---

## 9. Decision Velocity Doctrine

**Purpose:** Move with appropriate speed without sacrificing truth.

- **Analysis exists to enable action.** Investigation that never changes a decision is incomplete work.
- **Reversible decisions should not be delayed unnecessarily.** When harm is low and rollback is easy, decide and learn.
- **Repeated analysis without new evidence is waste.** The same question should not be re-litigated unless facts changed.
- **Documentation should reduce future work.** Records exist so the next person — human or automated — does not rediscover the same ground.
- **Decision velocity never overrides the Trust Hierarchy** on public buy guidance. Speed applies to internal and reversible choices, not to weakening fit or evidence standards.

---

## 10. User Experience Doctrine

**Purpose:** How trust feels to homeowners — survives redesigns and platform changes.

- **Evidence before store links** — explain what was found before pushing a retailer hop.
- **Confidence and uncertainty before the main action** — fit strength and gaps are visible, not buried.
- **Homeowner language on public surfaces** — no operator, pipeline, or internal quality-assurance jargon.
- **Calm utility** — plain language and restrained visual confidence; badges mean something; empty hype is banned.
- **Universal trust questions** — when applicable, every public page answers: what we found; what to compare; why this is relevant; what evidence supports it; what is uncertain; what to do next; whether buying options appear; why they are hidden if not; what to avoid.
- **Wrong-family prevention is a UX obligation** — distinct part families must not be collapsed in presentation.

---

## 11. Automation Doctrine

**Purpose:** How automated systems may operate without eroding trust.

- **Automation may discover; only validated gates may publish authority** — discovery status, workflow status, and truth status are not the same.
- **Production changes that affect public trust require explicit human authorization** for the specific action.
- **Automated systems inherit this Constitution** — they must not bypass the Trust Hierarchy or Truth Contract.
- **Fail closed on public buy guidance** — when automation is uncertain, default is withhold or UNKNOWN.
- **Operator richness stays internal** — dashboards and diagnostic labels are not homeowner copy.
- **No autonomous helpfulness that increases wrong-part risk** — filling compatibility gaps, skipping checks, or showing buy buttons to improve metrics is prohibited.

---

## 12. AI-Native Company Doctrine

**Purpose:** How BuckParts uses artificial intelligence and automation as a core operating model without surrendering accountability.

- **Automation should remove repetitive work** — not judgment that carries trust consequences.
- **Automation must not remove trust gates** — efficiency never deletes evidence, validation, or fail-closed defaults.
- **Human judgment remains accountable** — people and founders remain responsible for outcomes even when machines assist.
- **Trustworthy outcomes matter more than human effort** — the goal is correct homeowner decisions, not preserving manual labor for its own sake.
- **AI outputs are proposals until validated** — models do not close truth, authorize purchases on behalf of homeowners, or override the Truth Contract.

This doctrine extends §11; where they differ on trust, the stricter rule applies.

---

## 13. Founder Authority Doctrine

**Purpose:** Clarify how human authority interacts with truth.

- **The founder may override decisions** — priorities, sequencing, scope, and risk tolerance are legitimate leadership choices.
- **The founder may not override reality** — facts, evidence, and physical fit do not change because of authority.
- **Overrides change decisions, not facts** — a chosen path does not retroactively validate weak evidence or rewrite what is known.
- **Material trust-related overrides should be documented** — when public trust behavior diverges from default constitutional rules, the rationale and scope are recorded so future operators understand what changed and why.

Founder authority does not suspend §4 Trust Hierarchy on public buy guidance without explicit, documented exception.

---

## 14. What Never Changes

1. We help people avoid the wrong part.
2. We are not the seller.
3. We do not guess fit.
4. FULL truth or UNKNOWN — never partial confidence to homeowners.
5. No buying option without evidence that passes buy-path policy.
6. Monetization never outranks truth.
7. Homeowners never see operator or pipeline jargon.
8. Automation does not close truth without validation.
9. Wrong-part harm outweighs coverage, speed, and revenue.
10. Reality outranks opinion; evidence outranks memory.
11. Customer outcomes outrank internal elegance.
12. The founder may override decisions, not facts.

## Appendix A: Glossary (homeowner-safe)

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Part number** | Code printed on the filter or part you remove. |
| **Compatible replacement** | A part from a maker other than the appliance brand, marketed to replace a specific part number. |
| **Official / brand-made part** | A part sold as the appliance brand’s own replacement. |
| **Buying options** | Links to retailer pages BuckParts has checked against the part number — shown only when policy allows. |
| **UNKNOWN** | BuckParts does not yet have enough evidence to state a claim confidently on that page. |
